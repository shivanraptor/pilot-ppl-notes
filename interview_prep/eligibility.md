# Eligibility and Application — Cathay Cadet Pilot Programme

**Navigation:** [Interview hub](index.md) | [Home](../index.md)

> **Primary source:** [Cadet Pilot Programme — Cathay careers](https://careers.cathaypacific.com/en/careers/jobs/hong-kong/cadet-pilot-programme-29631) and [FAQ — interview process](https://careers.cathaypacific.com/en/faqs/hong-kong/pilot/cadet/what-is-the-interview-process-like).

## Who can apply (cadet)

| Item | Detail |
| --- | --- |
| **Hong Kong Permanent Identity Card** | The **Cadet Pilot Programme** has been advertised for holders of a **Hong Kong permanent ID**. Applicants without permanent ID are often directed to **Advanced Entry** or **Transition** programmes instead — read the live job post. |
| **Right to work** | Related FAQ: living/working in HK without permanent ID may affect eligibility — confirm with careers team. |
| **Age** | 18 years or above |
| **Education / background** | No flying hours required for cadet; strong academic and personal record expected |

---

## Programme overview (study context)

| Topic | Typical information (verify when you apply) |
| --- | --- |
| **Duration** | Often cited ~**80 weeks** sponsored pathway to CPL and airline training |
| **Outcome** | Second Officer pathway with Cathay Pacific |
| **Training** | Ground school (HK and partner institutions) + flight training with approved organisations (candidates report providers such as **FTA** and **Aeroguard** — confirm current contract partners) |
| **Bond / costs** | Sponsored training with service obligation; exact bond and cost offset are **application-specific** — prepare to discuss in initial interview |
| **Re-application** | If unsuccessful, check careers FAQ for waiting period before re-applying |

## English proficiency (pre-ICAO test)

One of the following (validity must cover your recruitment timeline):

| Test | Requirement (summary) |
| --- | --- |
| **IELTS** | Overall **6.0+** |
| **TOEIC (public)** | **800+** (Listening 400+, Speaking 160+) |
| **TOEFL iBT** (after 21 Jan 2026) | Total **4.5+** (Listening 4.5+, Speaking 4.5+) |
| **TOEFL iBT** (before 21 Jan 2026) | Total **80+** (Listening 21+, Speaking 21+) |

You must also achieve **ICAO English Level 4+** in the airline’s LPT — see [ICAO English](icao_english.md).

## Physical requirements (summary)

| Item | Detail |
| --- | --- |
| **HKCAD Class 1 medical** | Required for airline operations — see [medical](medical.md) |
| **Pre-employment drug test** | Safety-sensitive role; HKCAD **CAD373** psychoactive substance guidance |
| **Flight deck reach** | Static reach test and possible **simulator reach** assessment — know your aircraft-type reach standards when invited |
| **Spectacles** | See Cathay FAQ: [I wear spectacles, is that okay?](https://careers.cathaypacific.com/en/faqs/hong-kong/pilot/cadet/) |

## Application package (high impact)

| Asset | Why it matters |
| --- | --- |
| **CV** | Gaps and timeline will be challenged in interview (“why X not Y?”). |
| **Cover letter** | Recent candidates report detailed questions on the cover letter — proofread grammar and claims. |
| **Consistency** | Initial and final interviews may repeat motivation questions to test **consistent** answers. |

### Application checklist

- [ ] Confirm **permanent ID** and programme type (cadet vs advanced entry)
- [ ] English certificate within validity rules
- [ ] CV + cover letter reviewed by a second person
- [ ] Research fleet, hubs, cadet pathway, and **role of Second Officer**
- [ ] Prepare “why Cathay / why cadet / why not private CPL” answers
- [ ] Plan HK lifestyle and long-term commitment narrative (especially if relocating)

---

## PPL before applying?

Cathay FAQ: flying experience is **not required** for cadet. A PPL can show motivation but is not mandatory. If you hold a PPL, do not confuse **CASA** exam prep ([PPL notes](../casa_ppl_exam/ppl_toc.md)) with **cadet interview** technical prep ([technical](technical.md), [study map](study_map.md)).
