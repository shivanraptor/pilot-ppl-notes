## What Cathay requires (summary)

| Requirement | Detail |
| --- | --- |
| **HKCAD Class 1** | Airline medical certificate standard |
| **Pre-employment drug test** | Safety-sensitive role per **CAD373** Annex B guidance |
| **Flight deck reach** | Static reach and possible simulator reach — see [eligibility](eligibility.md) |
| **Background check** | Criminal records and employment verification |

Medical stage often occurs **after** interviews in some cycles; order may vary — follow your invitation letter.

---

## HKCAD Class 1 — what to expect

| Area | Typical assessment themes |
| --- | --- |
| **Vision** | Distance and near visual acuity; colour vision; spectacle correction limits — see Cathay FAQ on spectacles |
| **Hearing** | Conversational and tone thresholds |
| **Cardiovascular** | ECG, blood pressure, risk factors |
| **Neurological / psychiatric** | History disclosure; fitness for duty |
| **Substances** | Alcohol and drug policy; CAD373 screening |

**Do not self-diagnose disqualification.** If unsure, book an **aviation medical examiner** information session or preliminary assessment.

---

## CAD373 drug screening (awareness)

| Point | Action |
| --- | --- |
| Prescription medication | Disclose honestly on medical forms; bring documentation |
| Recreational drugs | Zero tolerance for safety-sensitive employment |
| OTC supplements | Know ingredients; avoid unlabelled products before test |

Read: [CAD373 PDF](https://www.cad.gov.hk/english/pdf/CAD373.pdf)

---

## Flight deck reach

| Test | Purpose |
| --- | --- |
| **Static reach** | Ensure you can reach overhead panels and controls per airline standard |
| **Simulator reach** | Confirm operational reach in seated position |

If you are borderline, ask careers/medical team what accommodations or measurement standards apply — do not assume failure without official measurement.

---

## Preparation checklist

- [ ] Download HKCAD medical guidance and read Class 1 sections  
- [ ] Gather past medical records if you have relevant history  
- [ ] Spectacles / contacts within corrected vision standards  
- [ ] Rest and hydration before audiometry  
- [ ] Honest disclosure — undisclosed history is a greater risk than treated conditions
