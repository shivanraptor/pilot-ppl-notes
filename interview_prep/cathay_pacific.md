## 1. History (milestones worth knowing)

| Year | Milestone |
| --- | --- |
| **1946** | Founded in Hong Kong by former wartime pilots **Roy Farrell** and **Sydney de Kantzow**. First aircraft: Douglas DC-3 **“Betsy”**. Name: *Cathay* (old name for China) + *Pacific* (ambition to cross the Pacific). |
| **1948** | **Swire** becomes key shareholder — partnership that still shapes governance. |
| **1959** | Acquires Hong Kong Airways; network expands (Japan, Australia, Taipei, etc.). |
| **1962–67** | Jet age — Convair 880; fleet becomes all-jet. |
| **1979–80** | First Boeing **747**; long-haul growth; passenger service to Chinese Mainland (Shanghai). |
| **1986–87** | Direct Beijing; **CITIC** becomes significant stakeholder. |
| **1998** | Move from **Kai Tak** to **Chek Lap Kok**; founding member of **oneworld**. |
| **2010s–20s** | A350 / 777 long-haul fleet; COVID rebuild; brand evolution to premium lifestyle. |
| **2023** | Master brand transformation completed; **Ronald Lam** as Group CEO (from Jan 2023). |
| **2024–26** | Capacity rebuild; 3RS at HKIA; large fleet orders (A330-900, 777-9, A350F, neo family); Chair succession to **Guy Bradley** (May 2026). |

Official narrative: [Our history](https://www.cathaypacific.com/cx/en_HK/about-us/about-our-airline/history.html).

**Interview tip:** One crisp arc — *Hong Kong–based hub carrier, Swire/Air China ownership, oneworld, rebuilding and investing in 3RS and next-gen fleet* — beats listing every year.

## 7. Cadet Pilot Programme

> **Always confirm** the live job post and FAQs — duration, partners, and bond terms change by intake.

**Careers hub:** [Cadet / pilot careers](https://careers.cathaypacific.com/en/careers/our-teams/pilot/pilot-detail-page) · selection detail on this site: [Eligibility](eligibility.md) · [Interview hub](index.md)

### Entry pathways (typical)

| Programme | Who it is for | Typical length (published examples) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Cadet Pilot Programme** | Little / **zero** experience; often requires **Hong Kong permanent ID** | Often advertised ~**80 weeks** end-to-end (confirm live post); earlier materials also cited ~55-week FTA flying phase within a longer pathway |
| **Advanced Entry** | ICAO **CPL** + hours (e.g. 250+ in specified categories) | Shorter (historically ~32 weeks — verify) |
| **Transition** | ICAO **ATPL** + substantial multi-engine time | Short conversion (historically ~5 weeks — verify) |

### Training flow (cadet — conceptual)

```text
Selection (app → English → aptitude → interviews → group → medical)
        ↓
Ground school (Hong Kong — culture, basics; partners have included Cathay Academy / PolyU-type partners)
        ↓
Ab initio flight training overseas (long-standing partner: Flight Training Adelaide / FTA;
  candidates also report other contracted FTOs in some cycles — confirm yours)
        ↓
CPL / MEIR + exams → return to Hong Kong as Second Officer
        ↓
Airline induction: MCC / multi-crew, ground school, full-flight simulator, line training
        ↓
Line Second Officer on Cathay fleet
```

**Reported FTA-era detail (study context):** classroom + ~**160** hours day/night flying (single + twin) and simulator hours toward CPL — exact syllabus is intake-specific.

Cathay story on FTA partnership: [Flight Training Adelaide & Cathay pilots](https://www.cathaypacific.com/cx/en_AU/inspiration/cathay-stories/flight-training-adelaide-cathay-pacific-pilots.html).

### Promotion path

Official careers FAQ wording (paraphrased): after the cadet programme you join as **Second Officer**, then progress by **experience, performance, vacancies, and seniority** to **First Officer**, then **Captain**.

| Rank | Role focus (interview level) | Progression notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Second Officer (SO)** | Cruise / relief support; multi-crew ops under SOP; building hours & type experience | Entry rank after cadet success |
| **First Officer (FO)** | PF/PM as qualified; expanding command potential | Timing **fleet-dependent** (demand & training slots) — historically often cited ~**3–4 years** but **not guaranteed** |
| **Captain** | Command; leadership & TEM | Typically requires substantial hours (industry commentary often cites **4,000+** hours context) and command course; commonly **10+ years** career path for cadet-entry — variable |

**Salary (published SO target — verify):** careers pages have quoted SO **target annual** figures on the order of **HKD ~629k** (Jan 2026 page wording) subject to target block hours — check live page.

### Mindset for panel

- Sponsored training → **service commitment / bond** understanding (ask careers for current terms; do not invent numbers).
- Goal is **airline standardisation**, not “hours only.”
- “We train future captains” — show long-term **Hong Kong** commitment and CRM maturity.
