## PPL chapters — use / skip for Cathay

| Chapter | Use for Cathay? | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Ch 1 Air Law](../casa_ppl_exam/ch1_air_law.md) | **Skip** for CX interview | Australia CASA/AIP — not HK cadet focus |
| [Ch 2 AGK](../casa_ppl_exam/ch2_aircraft_general_knowledge_agk.md) | **Partial** | §2.7 instruments; §2.6 gyros; ignore piston-only traps if question is jet |
| [Ch 3 Performance](../casa_ppl_exam/ch3_flight_performance_and_planning.md) | **Optional** | Balanced field, density altitude concepts |
| [Ch 4 Human factors](../casa_ppl_exam/ch4_human_performance_and_limitations.md) | **High** | TEM, CRM, illusions, fatigue — HR + group exercise |
| [Ch 5 Meteorology](../casa_ppl_exam/ch5_meteorology.md) | **Partial** | Fronts, METAR/TAF logic; skip Australian GAF emphasis |
| [Ch 6 Navigation](../casa_ppl_exam/ch6_navigation.md) | **Low** | Unless aptitude numeracy needs refresh |
| [Ch 7 Operational procedures](../casa_ppl_exam/ch7_operational_procedures.md) | **High** | SOP mindset, sterile cockpit, emergencies Aviate-Navigate-Communicate |
